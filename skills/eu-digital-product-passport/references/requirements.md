## Regulation (EU) 2024/1781

Source: http://publications.europa.eu/resource/oj/L_202401781.ENG

Regulation (EU) 2024/1781 of 13 June 2024 establishing a framework for the setting of ecodesign requirements for sustainable products. The fetched text sets the digital product passport rules inside that framework.

- **Article 3.** Products shall only be placed on the market or put into service if they comply with the ecodesign requirements applicable to those products, set out in the delegated acts adopted pursuant to Article 4.
- **text.** By 19 July 2026, the Commission shall set up a digital registry (the ‘registry’) which stores in a secure manner at least the unique identifiers.
- **text.** When preparing a delegated act to be adopted pursuant to paragraph 3, point (a), the Commission shall: (a) assess the prevalence and environmental impacts of the destruction of certain unsold consumer products;
- **Article 39.** The implementing acts referred to in the first subparagraph of this paragraph shall be adopted in accordance with the examination procedure referred to in Article 73(3).
- **Article 52.** The notification shall become valid the day after the body is included in the list of notified bodies referred to in Article 57(2) by the Commission.
- **text.** Before adopting a delegated act, the Commission shall consult experts designated by each Member State acting in accordance with the principles laid down in the Interinstitutional Agreement of 13 April 2016 on Better Law-Making.
