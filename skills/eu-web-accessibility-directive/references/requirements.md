## Directive (EU) 2016/2102

Source: https://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32016L2102.ENG

Directive (EU) 2016/2102 of 26 October 2016 on the accessibility of the websites and mobile applications of public sector bodies, published in Official Journal L 327 of 2 December 2016.

- **text.** They describe what must be achieved in order for the user to be able to perceive, operate, interpret and understand a website, a mobile application and related content.
- **Article 4.** Those technical specifications shall meet the accessibility requirements set out in Article 4 and shall ensure at least a level of accessibility equivalent to that ensured by European standard EN 301 549 V1.1.2 (2015-04).
- **Article 11.** By 23 December 2018, the Commission shall adopt the first such implementing act.
- **Article 4.** That report shall be drawn up on the basis of the arrangements for reporting referred to in paragraph 6 of this Article.
- **Article 11.** By 23 September 2018, Member States shall inform the Commission of the body responsible for the enforcement of this Directive.
- **Article 4.** Where reference is made to this paragraph, Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 shall apply.
