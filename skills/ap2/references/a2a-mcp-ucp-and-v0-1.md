# AP2 with UCP, A2A and MCP, and the v0.1 model

## Where AP2 sits

AP2 v0.2 is a security layer inside a commerce protocol. Catalog APIs, checkout updates and the messages between roles are out of scope (Spec, introduction). The Executive Summary describes AP2 as an extension for A2A, MCP and the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) (Overview, Section 2.1). The home page says it is available as an extension for A2A and UCP, with more integrations in progress.

### UCP

AP2 v0.2 is designed to be compatible with UCP (Spec, introduction). With UCP, `checkout_jwt` must be the UCP Checkout object (Checkout Mandate, Mandate Schema), and UCP's own extension points are the recommended way to support new commerce journeys (Spec, Checkout Object). The FAQ describes UCP as orchestrating the purchase lifecycle and AP2 as the payment layer that authorizes and signs transactions.

The UCP documentation describes the flow from its side:

1. The business's discovery document declares support for the AP2 extension.
2. The platform activates AP2 when creating or updating a checkout session.
3. The business answers with a `checkoutSignature`, a detached JWT over the checkout state, and lists the presentation formats it accepts, for example `sd-jwt`.
4. After user consent the platform creates a CheckoutMandate (with the hash of the checkout object) and a PaymentMandate (an SD-JWT VC).
5. The platform submits both to the business's `/complete` endpoint; the business verifies the CheckoutMandate and the payment processor verifies the PaymentMandate.

Source: UCP and AP2, Protocol flow. Field names on the UCP side follow UCP's own schema, not the AP2 tables.

### A2A

The v0.2 specification defines no A2A binding of its own. The Implementation Considerations page lists a merchant agent that talks A2A to the shopping agent as one way to structure the Merchant role. The v0.2 samples still use A2A: the sample merchant Agent Card declares the extension URI `https://github.com/google-agentic-commerce/ap2/v1` with `required: true` in `capabilities.extensions`. That URI comes from sample code, not from a normative document.

The only normative A2A binding is the v0.1 one below. If you build on A2A today, use the `a2a` skill for the transport, keep AP2 payloads as v0.2 SD-JWT mandates, and document the extension URI you advertise.

### MCP

The FAQ says an SDK and an MCP server are being built with payment service providers. The glossary notes MCP-based endpoints can play roles similar to a Payment Agent. There is no MCP binding in v0.2. The v0.1 topic page summarised the split as "MCP: agents communicate with data (APIs); A2A: agents communicate with other agents (tasks and messages); AP2: agents communicate about payments (mandates)" (v0.1 AP2, A2A, and MCP).

## The v0.1 model (superseded)

AP2 v0.1.0 was released on 2025-09-16. Its specification named three verifiable credentials (v0.1 specification, § 4.1):

- **Cart Mandate** (§ 4.1.1): captures the user's authorization when the human is present. Generated by the Merchant from the user's request and signed by the user, typically with a hardware-backed key. Binds payer and payee, a tokenized payment method, a risk payload, the exact products, destination, amount and currency, and refund conditions where applicable.
- **Intent Mandate** (§ 4.1.2): for when the human is not present. Generated by the Shopping Agent and signed by the user. May bind payer and payee, chargeable payment methods, a risk payload, the shopping intent (categories, SKUs, criteria such as refundability), a natural-language "prompt playback", and a time-to-live.
- **Payment Mandate** (§ 4.1.3): bound to the Cart or Intent Mandate and shared with the network and issuer. Always signals AI agent presence and the transaction modality.

### v0.1 A2A extension

The v0.1 A2A extension (`v0.1-alpha`) defined (A2A Extension for AP2):

- Extension URI `https://github.com/google-agentic-commerce/ap2/tree/v0.1`, advertised in `capabilities.extensions` of the Agent Card.
- `params.roles`: at least one of `merchant`, `shopper`, `credentials-provider`, `payment-processor`. Merchant agents should mark the extension required.
- **IntentMandate Message**: an A2A Message with a DataPart keyed `ap2.mandates.IntentMandate`, plus an optional `risk_data` DataPart.
- **CartMandate Artifact**: an A2A Artifact with a DataPart keyed `ap2.mandates.CartMandate`. A merchant must not produce it until all payment-impacting information (anything that changes the price, such as a shipping address) is collected.
- **PaymentMandate Message**: an A2A Message with a DataPart keyed `ap2.mandates.PaymentMandate`.

The examples use parts with `kind: "data"` and `role: "user"`. A2A 1.0 parts carry the content field (`data`) without `kind`, and roles are `ROLE_USER` and `ROLE_AGENT` (see the `a2a` skill), so these examples need adapting before use with an A2A 1.0 stack.

### Moving from v0.1 to v0.2

v0.2 defines exactly two mandate types, Checkout and Payment (Spec, Mandates). The v0.2 pages do not publish a mapping from the v0.1 names. The following is an inference from the two texts, so confirm it before you rely on it:

| v0.1                                      | Closest v0.2 construct                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Intent Mandate (user absent)              | Open Checkout Mandate and open Payment Mandate with constraints and `cnf`             |
| Cart Mandate (merchant cart, user-signed) | Closed Checkout Mandate over the merchant-signed `checkout_jwt`                       |
| Payment Mandate (network visibility)      | Closed Payment Mandate bound by `transaction_id`, verified by the CP, network and MPP |

Treat a v0.1 payload as a different protocol version: do not feed `ap2.mandates.*` objects into a v0.2 verifier, and do not accept v0.2 mandates where a v0.1 peer expects the old shapes.
