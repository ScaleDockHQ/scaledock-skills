## AODA 2005

Source: https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/05a11

- **AODA, s. 14 (1).** A person or organization to whom an accessibility standard applies shall file an accessibility report with a director annually or at such other times as the director may specify.
- **AODA, s. 14 (2).** A person or organization shall make an accessibility report filed under subsection (1) available to the public.
