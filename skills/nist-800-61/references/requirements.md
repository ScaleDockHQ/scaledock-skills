## SP 800-61 Rev 3

Source: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-61r3.pdf

- **§ 2.** Incident response is now considered a critical part of cybersecurity risk management that should be integrated across organizational operations.
- **§ 2.** The lessons learned during incident response should often be shared as soon as they are identified, not delayed until after recovery concludes.
- **ID.IM-01 R1.** Periodically evaluate incident response program performance to identify problems and deficiencies that should be corrected.
- **ID.IM-04 R1.** Synchronize business continuity plans with incident response plans since incidents can undermine business resilience.
- **DE.CM-09 R2.** Monitor authentication attempts to identify attacks against credentials and unauthorized credential use.
- **DE.AE-06 R2.** Make log analysis findings accessible to incident responders and other authorized personnel at all times.
- **DE.AE-08 R1.** Apply incident criteria to known and assumed characteristics of analyzed activity, and consider known false positives to determine whether an incident should be declared.
- **RS.MA-01 R1.** Detection technologies should automatically report incidents that they have confirmed.
- **RS.MA-02 R1.** Perform a preliminary review of a new incident report to verify that a cybersecurity incident has occurred, then estimate the severity of the incident and the level of urgency needed to respond to it.
- **RS.MA-02 R2.** Have mechanisms in place for third parties to report possible incidents that involve the organization.
- **RS.MA-03 R2.** Prioritize how quickly incident response should be performed for each incident based on its scope, likely impact, time-critical nature, and resource availability.
- **RS.AN-03 R3.** Analyze the incident to find the underlying or systemic root causes.
- **RS.AN-06 R1.** Safeguard the confidentiality and integrity of incident response records, and ensure that only authorized personnel have access.
- **RS.CO-02 R2.** Follow established procedures concerning incident coordination that include what must be reported to whom and at what times (e.g., initial notification, regular status updates).
- **RS.MI-02 R1.** Identify all affected hosts and services within the organization so that all flaws and weaknesses can be remediated.
- **RC.RP-03 R1.** Check restoration assets for indicators of compromise, file corruption, and other integrity issues before use.
- **RC.RP-05 R1.** Check restored assets for indicators of compromise, and remediate the root causes of the incident before production use.
- **RC.RP-06 R1.** Prepare an after-action report that documents the incident itself, the response and recovery actions taken, and lessons learned.
