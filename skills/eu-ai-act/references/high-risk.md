# High-risk requirements: Arts. 9 to 15, Annex IV and operator duties

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, consolidated text of 27.07.2026. These requirements apply to high-risk systems from 2 December 2027 (Annex III) or 2 August 2028 (Annex I) (Art. 113(c)). Not legal advice; harmonised standards and Commission guidance may set out how to meet each article.

The post-market monitoring plan is part of the Annex IV documentation, and the Commission adopts guidance with a template by 2 September 2027 (Art. 72(3)).

## Provider obligations (Art. 16)

Comply with Section 2 (Arts. 8 to 15); show name and contact address; run a quality management system (Art. 17); keep documentation (Art. 18) and logs under their control (Art. 19); complete conformity assessment before placing on the market (Art. 43); draw up an EU declaration of conformity (Art. 47); affix CE marking (Art. 48); register (Art. 49(1)); take corrective action (Art. 20); demonstrate conformity on request; meet accessibility requirements.

The quality management system (Art. 17(1)) covers among other things a regulatory compliance strategy, design and development controls, testing and validation procedures, technical specifications and standards, data management, risk management, post-market monitoring, serious incident reporting, record-keeping and an accountability framework.

## Deployer obligations (Arts. 26 and 27)

- Use the system per the instructions, with technical and organisational measures (26(1)).
- Assign human oversight to competent, trained people with authority and support (26(2)).
- Where the deployer controls input data, keep it relevant and sufficiently representative (26(4)).
- Monitor operation and inform the provider under Art. 72 (26(5)); keep logs under its control for at least six months (26(6)).
- Employers inform workers' representatives and affected workers before workplace use (26(7)).
- Annex III systems that make or assist decisions about natural persons: inform those persons (26(11)).
- Public bodies, private entities providing public services, and deployers of Annex III point 5(b) and (c) systems perform a fundamental rights impact assessment before deployment (27(1)).

## Post-market monitoring and serious incidents (Arts. 72 and 73)

- Providers run a post-market monitoring system based on a plan (72).
- Serious incidents are reported immediately after a causal link, or its reasonable likelihood, is established, and no later than 15 days after awareness (73(2)); no later than 2 days for a widespread infringement or a serious incident under Art. 3(49)(b), critical infrastructure (73(3)); no later than 10 days in the event of a death (73(4)).

Engineering consequence: incident detection, triage and reporting need to fit inside those windows, so link alerting and on-call to the reporting procedure.
