# Versions and upgrades

Read this when deciding which text of the AI Act to work from, when a document, plan or summary cites the original dates, or when a new amending act or consolidated version appears. Sources: the Official Journal text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the consolidated text of 27.07.2026, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, and the Publications Office metadata for the Regulation, listed in [Sources](../SKILL.md#sources). Not legal advice.

## Version lines

| Id                      | Line                              | Status  | Revision                                                      | Posture | Summary                                                                |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `consolidated-20260727` | consolidated text of 27 July 2026 | current | CELEX 02024R1689-20260727, incorporating Regulation 2026/1744 |         | The Regulation as amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI. Use its dates. |
| `original-20240712`     | original text of 12 July 2024     | legacy  | OJ L 2024/1689 of 12.7.2024 (CELEX 32024R1689)                |         | As adopted. Its high-risk dates and several articles were replaced.    |

Statuses: **current** is the default target; **supported** is released and still a valid target when a consumer needs it; **legacy** is superseded, read and upgraded from but never authored; **preview** is a draft of the next line, used only as its posture allows.

A line here is a text of the Regulation, not a software version. Only the Official Journal texts (the Regulation and each amending act) are authentic; a consolidated version is a documentation tool with no legal effect. The current line is therefore the original Regulation read with Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, for which the consolidated text of 27 July 2026 is the working copy.

The Publications Office lists exactly two consolidated versions, `02024R1689-20240712` (the original text) and `02024R1689-20260727`, so no intermediate consolidated version exists. It also lists one amending act, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, and four corrigenda to the Regulation, `32024R1689R(01)` to `32024R1689R(04)`, dated 9 October 2025, 19 December 2025, 27 March 2026 and 4 May 2026. The English consolidated text of 27.07.2026 lists only the Omnibus under "Amended by" and carries no corrigendum markers. This skill did not read the corrigenda; check them for the language version you rely on.

## Which version to use

- Work from the consolidated text of 27 July 2026, and cite the Official Journal text of the Regulation and of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 when an answer has legal weight.
- There is no supported line: the original text of 12 July 2024 is legacy. Read it only to understand older material and to correct it.
- Dates come from Art. 113 and Art. 111 of the consolidated text. A plan, summary or contract that cites 2 August 2026 for high-risk obligations or 2 August 2027 for Art. 6(1) follows the legacy text.
- There is no preview: see [Preview](#preview).

## What changed

The full list of Omnibus changes, the key dates and the transitional rules are in [`timeline.md`](timeline.md). The changes that matter most when moving material from the original text:

### consolidated text of 27 July 2026

- High-risk rules (Chapter III Sections 1 to 3, except Art. 6(5)) apply from 2 December 2027 for Art. 6(2) and Annex III systems and from 2 August 2028 for Art. 6(1) and Annex I systems (Art. 113(c)). Arts. 102 to 110 apply from 27 July 2026 (Art. 113(d)).
- New prohibitions on non-consensual intimate deepfakes and child sexual abuse material, applying from 2 December 2026 (Art. 5(1)(ba), (bb), Art. 5(1a) and (1b); Art. 113(a)).
- AI literacy becomes an obligation to take measures to support AI literacy (Art. 4), and bias-detection processing of special-category data moves to a new Art. 4a.
- Safety components are clarified (Art. 6(1a) to (1c)), and simplified technical documentation extends to small mid-cap enterprises (Art. 11).
- A transitional deadline of 2 December 2026 for Art. 50(2) marking by systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 (Art. 111(4)), and a revised code-of-practice adequacy mechanism (Art. 50(7)).
- Fines for small mid-cap enterprises are capped at the lower amount (Art. 99(6a)).

### original text of 12 July 2024

- Applied from 2 August 2026, with Chapters I and II from 2 February 2025, Chapter III Section 4, Chapter V, Chapter VII, Chapter XII and Art. 78 from 2 August 2025 (except Art. 101), and Art. 6(1) with its obligations from 2 August 2027 (Art. 113 of the OJ text).

## Upgrading

### original-20240712 to consolidated-20260727

Use this to bring a classification, requirements list, compliance plan or summary written against the original text up to date.

1. Change the version marker: cite "Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, consolidated text of 27.07.2026" (or the Regulation as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) wherever the document names its basis.
2. Replace changed provisions: move every date to the consolidated Art. 113 and Art. 111 (see the table in [`timeline.md`](timeline.md#key-dates)); re-screen Art. 5 for points (ba) and (bb); rewrite AI literacy work against the new Art. 4; move bias-detection data handling to Art. 4a; re-check safety-component status under Art. 6(1a) to (1c); and re-check Art. 11 eligibility for simplified documentation.
3. Validate against the target: re-read each cited article in the consolidated text and confirm the citation still points at the same obligation, then check the Publications Office for newer consolidated versions, amending acts and corrigenda.
4. Keep behaviour unchanged where the law did not change: a later deadline is not a reason to drop a control that is already built. Record which obligations moved and which stayed.

## Preview

None is listed. As of 2026-10-05 the Publications Office lists no amending act after Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 and no consolidated version after 27.07.2026. Proposals for amendments are not law and are not listed as previews; when a new amending act is published in the Official Journal and a new consolidated version appears, add it as the current line, make the 27 July 2026 text legacy, and add an upgrade section.
