The original Art. 113 applied the high-risk rules from 2 August 2026, and Art. 6(1) obligations from 2 August 2027. The Omnibus replaced those dates; older material that cites them is out of date.

## Systems already on the market (Art. 111)

- High-risk systems placed on the market or put into service before the Chapter III application date are covered only if their designs change significantly from that date. Providers and deployers of high-risk systems intended for public authorities have their own deadline in Art. 111(2); read it in full.
- GPAI models placed before 2 August 2025: comply by 2 August 2027 (111(3)).
- Synthetic-content generators placed before 2 August 2026: comply with Art. 50(2) by 2 December 2026 (111(4)).

## What the Digital Omnibus on AI changed

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of 8 July 2026, published on 24.7.2026, amends the AI Act. Among its changes, as reflected in the consolidated text and the Commission page:

- New high-risk dates of 2 December 2027 and 2 August 2028 (Art. 113(c)).
- New prohibitions on non-consensual intimate and sexual deepfakes and on child sexual abuse material, from 2 December 2026 (Art. 5(1)(ba), (bb)).
- AI literacy rewritten as an obligation to take measures to support AI literacy, without guaranteeing a specific level (Art. 4).
- Bias-detection processing of special-category data moved to a new Art. 4a.
- Clarification of which systems are safety components (Art. 6(1a) to (1c)).
- Simplified technical documentation extended to small mid-cap enterprises (Art. 11).
- A transitional deadline for Art. 50(2) (Art. 111(4)) and a revised code-of-practice adequacy mechanism (Art. 50(7)).
- Stronger AI Office powers and centralised oversight of AI systems built on GPAI models (Commission page).

## Penalties (Art. 99)

| Infringement                                                                                          | Maximum fine                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prohibited practices (Art. 5)                                                                         | EUR 35 000 000 or, for an undertaking, 7 % of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher |
| Other operator obligations listed in Art. 99(4), including provider, deployer and Art. 50 obligations | EUR 15 000 000 or 3 %, whichever is higher                                                         |
| Incorrect, incomplete or misleading information to notified bodies or authorities                     | EUR 7 500 000 or 1 %, whichever is higher                                                          |

For SMCs, the Omnibus caps each fine under Art. 99(4) and (5) at the percentage or amount, whichever is lower (Art. 99(6a)). Fines for GPAI model providers are in Art. 101.

## Before relying on a date

Re-read Art. 113 in the newest consolidated version, and check for later amending acts and corrigenda. Four corrigenda to the original Regulation are listed by the Publications Office; this skill did not read them.
