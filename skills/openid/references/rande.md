# R&E Working Group: Research and Education

There is no dedicated skill for this family, and the working group has published nothing yet.

The R&E working group is creating profiles of OpenID Connect to ease adoption in the research and education sector. The profiles will include international standards for representing users of R&E institutions and the existing trust fabric of R&E identity federations and multilateral trust exchange.
