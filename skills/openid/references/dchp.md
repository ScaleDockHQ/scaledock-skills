# Digital Credentials Harmonized Presentation (DCHP) Working Group

There is no dedicated skill for this family, and the working group has published nothing yet.

The working group supports a joint initiative by ISO/IEC JTC1/SC 17 WG10 and WG4 and the OpenID Foundation DCP working group to harmonise their credential exchange protocols for in-person and online credential presentation. Its repository is <https://github.com/openid/dchp>.

Index: [Digital Credentials Harmonized Presentation Working Group – Specifications](https://openid.net/wg/digital-credentials-harmonized-presentation-working-group/specifications/), checked 2026-10-02.
