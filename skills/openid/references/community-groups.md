# OpenID Foundation community groups

Community groups bring in other foundations, consortiums and international collaborators to work on a proof of concept, an industry-wide problem or a technology, and deliver documents or working concepts. They do not publish OpenID specifications; protocol work goes to a working group. There is no dedicated skill.

Index: [Community Groups](https://openid.net/cg/) and [Archived Groups](https://openid.net/wg/archived-groups/), checked 2026-10-02. Each group page below was read on 2026-10-02.

## Active community groups

| Group                                                                                                                             | Focus                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Repository                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Artificial Intelligence Identity Management](https://openid.net/cg/artificial-intelligence-identity-management-community-group/) | A space to assess use cases, modularisation, AI architectures and gaps in standards for agentic AI, such as how to assert the identity of an LLM or agent to external servers and what a token passed between AI agents contains. It informs an OIDF whitepaper. Developing protocols is out of scope and deferred to working groups or liaison partners. | <https://github.com/openid/cg-ai-identity-management>    |
| [Australian Digital Trust](https://openid.net/cg/australian-digital-trust-community-group/)                                       | A neutral platform for professionals working on Australian digital identity and trust ecosystems.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | <https://github.com/openid/cg-australian-digital-trust>  |
| [Death and the Digital Estate (DADE)](https://openid.net/cg/death-and-the-digital-estate/)                                        | Use cases for an individual's right to choose what happens to their digital data on death or disablement, with a long-term aim of a working group for protocols.                                                                                                                                                                                          | <https://github.com/openid/death-and-the-digital-estate> |
| [Ecosystem Support](https://openid.net/cg/ecosystem-support-community-group/)                                                     | Supports open data ecosystems as they implement and profile OIDF standards, including an Open Banking Reference Technical Architecture and best practices document (version 0.1).                                                                                                                                                                         | <https://github.com/openid/cg-ecosystem-support>         |

## When to use this file

Use it to point users to the right forum, for example AI agent identity questions to the AI Identity Management group. For anything to implement, use the working group references.
