# iGov Working Group: International Government Assurance Profile

There is no dedicated skill for this family. This file is the reference.

The iGov working group develops a security and privacy profile of OpenID Connect so users can authenticate and share consented attributes with public sector services across jurisdictions, including through identity brokers.

Index: [iGov Working Group – Specifications](https://openid.net/wg/igov/specifications/), checked 2026-10-02. Dates come from each document's header, read on 2026-10-02.

## Implementer's Drafts

### International Government Assurance Profile (iGov) for OAuth 2.0

- Implementer's Draft listed by the working group: [openid-igov-oauth2-1_0-ID1.html](https://openid.net/specs/openid-igov-oauth2-1_0-ID1.html), titled "Draft 03", October 5, 2018.
- Unversioned URL: [openid-igov-oauth2-1_0.html](https://openid.net/specs/openid-igov-oauth2-1_0.html), titled "1.0 Implementers Draft 2", 13 April 2026. The working group page still names ID1 as the most recent Implementer's Draft; confirm with the working group before pinning the newer document as an approved Implementer's Draft.
- What it defines: an OAuth 2.0 profile that raises baseline security, improves interoperability and structures deployments, aimed at but not limited to consumer-to-government services.
- When to implement: authorization servers, clients and resource servers in government deployments that adopt iGov.

### International Government Assurance Profile (iGov) for OpenID Connect 1.0

- Implementer's Draft listed by the working group: [openid-igov-openid-connect-1_0-ID1.html](https://openid.net/specs/openid-igov-openid-connect-1_0-ID1.html), titled "Draft 03", October 05, 2018.
- Unversioned URL: [openid-igov-openid-connect-1_0.html](https://openid.net/specs/openid-igov-openid-connect-1_0.html), draft 04, 3 August 2023.
- What it defines: an OpenID Connect profile for government and public service domains. It refers to the iGov OAuth 2.0 profile for authorization endpoint and registration requirements.
- When to implement: OPs and RPs in government deployments that adopt iGov.
