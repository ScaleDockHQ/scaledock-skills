# Conformity assessment, documentation, CE marking and dates

Sources: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Arts. 13, 27 to 33, 35, 64, 69 to 71; Annexes V to VIII), Regulation (EU) 2025/327 (Arts. 104, 105), the Commission FAQs version 1.4 (section 6), the Commission policy and reporting pages and the Commission guidance C(2026) 5252. Not legal advice; counsel and, where required, a notified body decide conformity.

| Critical (Annex IV)                        | A European cybersecurity certificate under Art. 8(1); where no delegated act requires one, any class II procedure (Art. 32(4); Art. 8(1)). A European cybersecurity certificate at assurance level at least "substantial" under a scheme specified by delegated act removes the third-party assessment obligation for the requirements it covers (Art. 27(9)). A manufacturer can always choose a stricter procedure (guidance footnote 23). Notified body fees take account of micro, small and medium-sized enterprises (Art. 32(6)).

### The modules (Annex VIII)

- **Module A (Part I).** The manufacturer draws up the Annex VII documentation, ensures design, development, production and vulnerability handling meet Annex I Parts I and II, affixes the CE marking to each product and draws up the declaration of conformity, on its sole responsibility. No notified body (FAQ 6.1).
- **Module B (Part II).** A single notified body of the manufacturer's choice examines the technical documentation, supporting evidence and specimens, issues an EU-type examination certificate covering the type and the vulnerability handling processes, and carries out periodic audits of the Part II processes (points 3 to 8). The manufacturer informs the body of modifications that may affect conformity, which need an addition to the certificate (point 7).
- **Module C (Part III).** Production conforms to the approved type; the manufacturer affixes the CE marking and draws up the declaration for each product model.
- **Module H (Part IV).** The manufacturer runs a quality system approved by a notified body for design, development, final inspection, testing and vulnerability handling, maintained through the support period, with periodic audits; the CE marking is followed by the notified body's identification number.

Notified bodies are notified under Chapter IV, which applies from 11 June 2026 (Art. 71(2)); the Commission publishes the list (Art. 44(2)), and Member States strive to have enough of them by 11 December 2026 (Art. 35(2)).

## Technical documentation (Art. 31, Annex VII)

Draw it up before placing on the market and keep it updated, where appropriate, at least during the support period (Art. 31(2)). It contains at least:

| Point | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | General description: intended purpose; software versions affecting compliance; for hardware, photographs or illustrations of external features, marking and internal layout; the Annex II user information.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 2     | Design, development, production and vulnerability handling: (a) design and development information, including system architecture showing how software components build on or feed into each other; (b) the vulnerability handling processes, including the SBOM, the coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy, evidence of the reporting contact address and the technical solution for secure update distribution; (c) production and monitoring processes and their validation. |
| 3     | The cybersecurity risk assessment under Art. 13, including how Annex I Part I applies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 4     | The information used to determine the support period under Art. 13(8).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 5     | Harmonised standards, common specifications or certification schemes applied in full or in part (naming the parts), or descriptions of the solutions used to meet Annex I Parts I and II.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6     | Reports of the tests verifying conformity of the product and of the vulnerability handling processes with Annex I Parts I and II.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 7     | A copy of the EU declaration of conformity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 8     | Where applicable, the SBOM, on reasoned request of a market surveillance authority.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

- High-risk AI systems under Art. 12 that are also subject to other Union acts requiring technical documentation use a single set (Art. 31(3)); from 26 March 2027 this also covers EHR systems under Art. 32(5a) (Regulation (EU) 2025/327, Art. 104).
- Microenterprises and small enterprises may use a simplified format through a form the Commission adopts by implementing act (Art. 33(5)); none is listed as of 2026-10-05.
- Keep the documentation and the declaration for at least 10 years after placing on the market or for the support period, whichever is longer (Art. 13(13)).
- For products designed before 11 December 2027 and placed afterwards, the guidance says test evidence need not cover the original design phases, and tests can be grouped across product families (guidance points 37, 38).

## EU declaration of conformity (Art. 28, Annexes V and VI)

- States that the applicable Annex I requirements are met, follows the Annex V structure, is kept up to date and translated as the Member State requires (Art. 28(1), (2)). By drawing it up, the manufacturer assumes responsibility for compliance (Art. 28(4)).
- Annex V content: product identification; manufacturer or authorised representative name and address; a statement of sole responsibility; the object of the declaration (traceable identification, optionally a photograph); a statement of conformity with the relevant Union harmonisation legislation; references to harmonised standards, common specifications or certifications used; where applicable, the notified body's name and number, the procedure and the certificate; additional information, place, date, name, function and signature.
- Where several Union acts require a declaration, draw up a single one naming them all (Art. 28(3)).
- The product is accompanied by a copy of the declaration or by the simplified declaration of Annex VI, which gives the exact internet address of the full text (Art. 13(20); Art. 28(2)).

## CE marking (Arts. 29 and 30)

- Affix it visibly, legibly and indelibly to the product; where that is not possible, to the packaging and the declaration. For software, affix it to the declaration or to the website accompanying the software, in a section easily and directly accessible to consumers (Art. 30(1)).
- It may be smaller than 5 mm if still visible and legible (Art. 30(2)), and is affixed before placing on the market (Art. 30(3)).
- Under module H, it is followed by the notified body's identification number (Art. 30(4)).
- The Commission may lay down labels, pictograms or other marks on product security and support periods by implementing act (Art. 30(6)).
- Open-source software stewards may not affix it (recital 19).

### Products already on the market (Art. 69)

- Placed on the market before 11 December 2027: subject to the CRA only if substantially modified from that date (Art. 69(2)), but Art. 14 applies to them all (Art. 69(3)).
- New units of an existing model placed on the market after that date are new placings and must comply (guidance points 33 to 37; FAQ 4.5.3).

## Penalties (Art. 64)

| Infringement                                                                                          | Maximum fine                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Annex I essential requirements and Arts. 13 and 14                                                    | EUR 15 000 000 or, for an undertaking, 2.5 % of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher (Art. 64(2)) |
| Arts. 18 to 23, 28, 30(1) to (4), 31(1) to (4), 32(1) to (3), 33(5), 39, 41, 47, 49 and 53            | EUR 10 000 000 or 2 %, whichever is higher (Art. 64(3))                                                           |
| Incorrect, incomplete or misleading information to notified bodies or market surveillance authorities | EUR 5 000 000 or 1 %, whichever is higher (Art. 64(4))                                                            |

Under Art. 64(10), as corrected by corrigendum R(02) to cover paragraphs 2 to 9, these fines do not apply to microenterprises or small enterprises for missing the 24-hour early warning deadlines in Art. 14(2)(a) or (4)(a), nor to any infringement by open-source software stewards. Member States lay down the penalty rules (Art. 64(1)).

## Before relying on a date or procedure

Re-read Art. 71 and Art. 32 in the Official Journal text, check the Publications Office for amending acts, corrigenda, a delegated act under Art. 8(1) and references of harmonised standards, and check the Commission's list of delegated and implementing acts (Art. 26(2)).
