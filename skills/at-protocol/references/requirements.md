## AT Protocol

Source: https://atproto.com/specs/atp

The Authenticated Transfer Protocol ("AT Protocol" or "atproto") is a network protocol for building open social web applications.

- **Protocol Structure.** AT Protocol itself does not specify how common social web conventions like follows or avatars should work, and instead leaves them to application designers.
- **What is Missing?.** Non-Public Data: mechanisms for personal and group data sharing are planned, and should support a wide variety of application functionality.
